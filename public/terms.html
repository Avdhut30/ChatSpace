Terms of responsible use

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Chatspace is provided as an independent open-source communication service. Use it only for lawful conversations and content you have the right to share.

Prohibited activity

  • Phishing, fraud, impersonation, malware, or deceptive downloads.
  • Harassment, threats, exploitation, or illegal content.
  • Attempting to access another member's account or private rooms.
  • Abusing uploads, automated traffic, or authentication systems.

Account security

Protect your password and use accurate account information. Chatspace will never ask for payment-card numbers, remote device access, or a software installation in order to use the web application.

Project identity

Chatspace is not affiliated with Telegram, WhatsApp, Google, or Microsoft. Product names belonging to others remain their respective owners' property.
